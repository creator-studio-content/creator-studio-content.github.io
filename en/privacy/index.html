Who we are

Orca ("we", "us") is a content planning and scheduling service operated from Cairo, Egypt. Contact us about privacy at orca.studio.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

We collect only the platform data needed to publish and show status. We do not collect your followers, private messages or other people’s content.

Data from social platforms

When you connect an account (for example Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn or X), the platform shares only what you approve on its consent screen. We use it only to publish the posts you schedule and to show their status, as each platform’s developer terms require. You can disconnect at any time in the app or in the platform’s settings; we then delete the stored access token.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How we use your data

To provide the service you asked for (our contract with you), to keep it secure and fix problems (our legitimate interest), and to meet legal duties. We don’t sell your data and we don’t use your content to advertise to you.

If you use optional AI writing help, your text is sent to our AI provider only to produce suggestions, under a contract that forbids using it for model training.

Who we share it with

The social platforms you publish to; service providers who host and run the service for us (cloud hosting, storage, email, AI help, payments) under contracts that protect your data; and authorities when the law requires it. Your data may be processed outside Egypt, with the safeguards required by Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Law and, for people in the EU, the GDPR.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data. Workspace owners can export a workspace’s data (delivered within 72 hours) or delete the workspace in the app; personal data and stored credentials are erased within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep. See Data deletion or write to orca.studio.app@gmail.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Platform tokens are encrypted with keys only the publishing service can use, and every use is logged. Access inside our team is limited to what each person needs.

Children

The service is not meant for children under 16 and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We’ll post changes here and, for important ones, tell account holders by email before they take effect.
